2. Convert Brow Clients Into Skin Clients

Hundreds of people already trust Browskii every month. The KPI is brow-to-skin conversion. Ten percent of 500 brow visits is 50 skin clients with zero ad spend.

3. Build Aglow Into Recurring Revenue

A 12-month skin journey at $300 a month. 25 members is $7,500 a month, 50 is $15,000, 100 is $30,000, and it is predictable before the month starts.

4. Proper Lead Generation and Follow-Up

Right now an ad sends someone straight to the Acuity calendar. If she books, great. If she does not, she is gone, and nobody knows she was ever there. The fix is a path with a catch at every step: the ad lands on a page about her skin; she leaves her name for the assessment or a consult; the CRM texts and emails her within a minute and keeps following up until she books; and every lead, booked or not, sits in one list you can see. Then a $300 ad spend that produced three bookings and twelve "not yets" is a list to work.

5. Quarterly VIP Nights With a Revenue Target

Browskii After Dark, once a quarter. First one: 40 to 60 guests, $20K+ in sales and bookings, 10+ memberships, 20+ future appointments. Then benchmark every event against the last.
