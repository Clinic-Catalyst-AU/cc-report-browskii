Quick wins - things you can do this week

The plan

Three marketing moves. Three systems. That's the whole thing.

Clinics stall when they try to do fifteen things. This is the six that matter for Browskii and MOMO this year.

Marketing

1. The rosacea funnel on Meta

A lead-form campaign, not a "straight to bookings" one. Concern-led hooks, the UGC story, a skin discovery session as the offer, and every lead landing in a CRM where it gets followed up. Run under MOMO so the brow audience isn't confused.

2. Google that finally shows Newstead you exist

Your site was built five years ago and has only had pages added since. We refresh the SEO foundations so "Newstead skin treatment" and "Newstead injectables" start returning you, and set up Google Ads for people already searching cosmetic tattoo, lip blush and skin in Brisbane.

3. A MOMO landing page in the new brand

Kelly is mocking up a concern-led landing page in your new burgundy and blue identity so you can see what the skin journey looks like when the website matches the brand. This is the page the ads land on.

Systems

1. A CRM with every lead tracked

Right now ads go straight to Acuity and nobody knows who enquired and didn't book. Every lead, DM and form gets a record, a follow-up sequence and a booked-or-not status. You see it on one screen.

2. An assistant on your inbox and DMs

Whoever-gets-to-it-first becomes a trained assistant in your voice that answers the FAQs already on your site, pre-frames the deposit and late-arrival policy, and sends the booking link. Sundays stop being yours.

3. Rebooking and product on autopilot

Your price list already has the refresh windows (6 to 12 months, 13 to 18 months). The system chases them. It also knows when a client's product runs out and invites them back in, and it sends the review request after every appointment.
